NAudio is used only for selecting and capturing the intended Windows microphone.

Vosk 0.3.38 (and libvosk native library)
Copyright Alpha Cephei Inc.
License: Apache License 2.0
Source: https://github.com/alphacephei/vosk-api

Vosk provides the free offline speech recognition engine. It bundles the
MinGW runtime libraries libgcc_s_seh-1.dll, libstdc++-6.dll, and
libwinpthread-1.dll (GCC Runtime Library Exception / MIT-like licenses).

vosk-model-small-en-us-0.15
License: Apache License 2.0
Source: https://alphacephei.com/vosk/models

The small English model performs the on-device speech recognition. It is
bundled unmodified and can be swapped for a larger model for more accuracy.

Piper 2023.11.14-2
Copyright 2022 Michael Hansen
License: MIT
Source: https://github.com/rhasspy/piper

Piper provides the free local neural text-to-speech engine. The archived MIT
release is bundled as an independent executable with its required libraries.

Piper voice: en_GB-cori-high
Model repository license: MIT
Training recordings: LibriVox public domain recordings
Source: https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/tree/main/en/en_GB/cori/high

The Cori voice is a single-speaker UK English voice trained from scratch. It
was selected instead of voice models whose training data prohibit commercial
use.
